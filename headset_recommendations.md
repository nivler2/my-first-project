## Quick recommendation

If you want the safest sub-$100 buy for mixed use (calls + music), pick **Soundcore Space One at $69.99**.

If your #1 priority is your voice quality on calls, prioritize a **wired boom-mic model** (Jabra Evolve2 30 when in stock, otherwise **Poly Blackwire 3220**).

## Extra tip (free)

In Zoom, keep background noise suppression enabled (usually **Auto/Low**) and avoid “Original Sound” unless needed for music. This can further reduce barking/background noise in addition to headset mic processing.
